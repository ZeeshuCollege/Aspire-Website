# ASPIRE Learning Centre — Website PRD

**Document:** Product Requirements Document  
**Product:** ASPIRE Learning Centre Official Website  
**Business Type:** Offline Coaching Institute  
**Primary Audience:** Prospective students and parents  
**Relationship with App:** Public-facing website + entry point into the private ASPIRE app ecosystem  
**Courses:** Std. 9, Std. 10, Std. 11, Std. 12, NEET, JEE Main & JEE Advanced

---

## 1. Product Overview

ASPIRE Learning Centre's website will be the institute's official digital presence for attracting new students, explaining courses, showcasing the institute's teaching approach and results, answering parent/student questions, generating admission enquiries, and providing access to the private ASPIRE app.

The website is **not the same product as the ASPIRE mobile app**.

- **Website:** Public-facing institute website for discovery, information, trust, enquiries and admissions.
- **App:** Private ecosystem for enrolled ASPIRE students, teachers, parents and admins.

The website should communicate that ASPIRE is an **offline coaching centre**. It should not present the institute as a generic online-learning marketplace.

---

# 2. Product Goals

1. Establish a professional and trustworthy online identity for ASPIRE Learning Centre.
2. Clearly explain every course and the students it is designed for.
3. Convert visitors into admission enquiries.
4. Make it easy for parents to contact the institute.
5. Showcase faculty, teaching methodology, infrastructure and verified results.
6. Provide clear information about batches, schedules, centre location and admissions.
7. Connect existing users to the private ASPIRE app.
8. Build a strong SEO foundation for local and exam-related searches.
9. Make the site fast, responsive and excellent on mobile.
10. Give ASPIRE staff a simple way to update important content.

# 22. Design Requirements

## Design Direction

The website should be:

**Modern + Minimal + Premium + Educational + Trustworthy + Dynamic**

Do not make it look like:
- a generic school website
- an old coaching website
- a template
- an AI-generated landing page

## Visual Language

Use:
- Strong editorial typography
- A distinctive type scale
- Clean white/light backgrounds
- ASPIRE brand colours
- Controlled secondary accent colours
- Sharp and rounded components mixed intentionally
- Subtle borders
- Soft shadows
- Light gradients only where useful
- High-quality imagery
- Large but disciplined whitespace
- Micro-interactions

Avoid:
- excessive glassmorphism
- excessive rounded cards
- excessive gradients
- repetitive pill-shaped components
- excessive animations
- huge blocks of text
- overly generic AI-style layouts

---

# 23. Typography

Use a deliberate typography system.

Example hierarchy:

**Display:** distinctive modern sans/serif combination  
**H1:** strong editorial heading  
**H2:** compact section heading  
**Body:** highly readable sans-serif  
**Labels:** smaller medium-weight typography  
**Numbers:** tabular/semibold typography for statistics

Typography should vary by purpose while remaining coherent.

# 40. Research-Informed Design Notes

The PRD was informed by reviewing currently hosted coaching/education products including PW Vidyapeeth, Aakash, Unacademy and Vedantu.

Observed useful patterns include:

- Offline centre pages that surface programmes, centre details, contact information, schedules/support and enrolment-related information.
- Course pages that clearly explain who a programme is for, duration/validity, testing and preparation features.
- Strong use of results, faculty/educator information, structured course presentation and support features.
- Test-series pages with explicit test counts, formats and performance-analysis information.
- Dedicated centre/location pages for parents and students researching local coaching.
- Clear calls to action for counselling, admission and course discovery.

ASPIRE should borrow these **information-architecture patterns**, while keeping its visual identity and content completely original.

# 45. Final Product Direction

The final website should feel like a **premium local coaching institute's official digital home**, not a copied version of a large ed-tech company's website.

Its personality should be:

**Academic • Ambitious • Trustworthy • Modern • Focused • Human**

The website should answer three questions quickly:

### 1. What does ASPIRE teach?
Std. 9–12, NEET, JEE Main & Advanced.

### 2. Why should a student/parent learn more?
Offline classroom teaching, faculty, structured preparation, regular testing, academic support and measurable progress.

### 3. What should they do next?
**Enquire → Contact → Visit → Admission**

The private app then takes over the day-to-day student, teacher, parent and admin ecosystem.
